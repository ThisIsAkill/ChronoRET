burn-down is finished: all routines meet [STYLE.md](STYLE.md) and `tools/readability_baseline.txt`
is empty. Banks $00 and $FD are reviewed except MainInit's last wording fix; bank $C1 is under

## Recent results

- Bank $C1 matched on from the targeting system: the frame wait `BattleSys_PumpFrames`
  ($C1:007E), battler boxes and their collision tests against the screen cell map and each other
  ($C1:283D–$C1:2985), the position queries of service 5 of the $C10045 API (nearest/farthest
  battler, distance checks, path check, screen-half tests; $C1:2986–$C1:2D9E), and the PC
  animation tick with the facing update ($C1:2D9F–$C1:2F96). Found along the way: the path
  check measures its distance from a stale X (an arbitrary RAM pair, not the target), kept as
  found; `$C1:2B07`/`$C1:2B13` are byte-identical twins; `$C1:2F1F` is an unreachable JMP to an RTS.

- Matching resumed: the battler frame decoder ($C1:1C4A–$C1:1F78, tiles copied by number with a
  horizontal-mirror bit), service 3 of the $C10045 API (`BattleSys_UpkeepTwoFrames`,
  $C1:106E–$C1:10E2), and the byte-identical copies of `Battle_Mul8` in bank $CC and of the wave
  tables in bank $FF (new bank files `bankCC` and `bankFF`).

- Readability burn-down finished: banks $C0 and $C1 brought up to STYLE.md (bytes unchanged,
  verified). Along the way: bare `Sub_XXXX` routines in bank $C0 got descriptive names, two
  mis-split instruction listings were corrected, and `$7E:2980-2982` was settled as the party's
  character ids (bank $C0's old "colour vars" reading was wrong).

- The battle targeting system is matched end to end ($C1:1F79–$C1:283C): the 33-entry target-mode
  table and its 18 handlers, the area-query service, the candidate-list helpers, and the four
  area shapes (row band, party triangle, line, circle).
- `BattleMenu_EnqueueReadyBattler` (service 1 of the $C10045 API) puts a battler in the
  ATB-ready queue and plays the turn-ready cue.
- The readability gate: `tools/lint_readability.py` per routine, `tools/verify.py` proves every
  emitted byte against blank bases, `tools/progress.py` generates every number, reviews are
  recorded in `symbols/reviews.csv`.

## Known issues

- Hand-maintained totals in older notes overstated bank $C0 by 169 bytes; the generated numbers
  above are the correct ones.
- Two asar notes from earlier sessions were wrong: `.b`/`.w` alone reproduces the encodings
  that were hand-written as `db`, and `BRL` accepts a label.

## Open questions

- `GameLoop` ($C0:000E) is probably misnamed: it starts by installing the NMI/IRQ trampolines
  (an init sequence), per the independent review of MainInit.
- The two cross-bank message vectors `$CD002D` / `$CD0030` used by the enemy info panel.

## Decisions

- 2026-10-08: routine headers are checked mechanically. `HEADER`: an Entry and an Exit line
  (tables exempt; sub-entries point at their parent with `; header: see <Parent>`). `CALLERS`:
  every caller site `tools/xref.py` confirms is named in the header (address, containing
  routine, or a site count). Requested by the maintainer after review rounds kept rejecting
  routines for missing Entry/Exit lines and incomplete caller lists. The existing headers were
  brought up to it in a separate commit first; that edit changed 112 routines' source hashes, so
  their reviews no longer count until they are re-reviewed.

- 2026-10-08: jumps and calls (`JSR`, `JMP`, `JSL`, `JML`, branches) are exempt from the
  explicit-width rule; each has exactly one encoding, so its width can't drift with how a define
  is written. Approved by the maintainer after an independent review flagged that the exemption
  had been added inside a code revision. From now on, standard changes are their own commit
  (CONTRIBUTING.md, "Changing the standard").
