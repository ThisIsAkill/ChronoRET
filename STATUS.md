## Recent results

- Bank $C1 matched on through service 4 and the action loaders ($C1:4058–$C1:49FE, 2,471 bytes):
  `BattleSys_RunAction` (service 4: plays one action's script frame by frame until it ends, then
  re-picks every status animation and draws the KO frame of PCs whose pending hit reaches their
  HP), its frame helpers (battler action animations, the per-entry calculations, the lethal-hit
  check), `BattleAct_LoadScript` and the four loaders it dispatches on `!Battle_ActKind` (none,
  attack, tech, kind 3: records in bank $CD, script thread masks and offsets in $CE/$CD), the
  common tail (graphics sets, unpacking the sprite frames through the $C3:0002 vector into
  `$7E:2D00`, 16 action objects), the object-list splitter and the sprite-frame builder. "Attack"
  and "tech" are guesses from the record layouts. Found along the way: three ROM bytes at
  `$CF:FFFD-FFFF` (all 0) can force the action flags; thread flags are stored 16-bit, spilling
  into the next byte; the second-group test is 16-bit while only its low byte is cleared; the
  loaders widen 8-bit values without clearing B; the "Near" PC attack path ends with a BRA to the
  next instruction; the frame header's high nibble is stored and never read; lists 1-15 of the
  object records never test their first record; a redundant kind-0 test and a reload of the same
  facing.

- Bank $C1 matched on through the enemy movers ($C1:3714–$C1:4057, 2,372 bytes): the per-frame
  tick (`Battle_TickEnemyMovers`, formerly the misnamed stub `Battle_TickStatusEffectVisuals`), the
  dispatch on `!Enemy_Anim` through `!BattleRom_EnemyMover` and the 9-entry
  `Battle_EnemyMoverTable`, and the nine movers: idle (resume when the target moves away), approach
  to 32/64/4 pixels with quarter-turn detours, charge until blocked, line up on one axis, keep to a
  ring round the target, orbit the target, loop round an ellipse, fixed directions, and head for the
  screen centre. Each picks the heading of the next 8-pixel step and tests the box there; the
  stepper at `$CF:F978` (unmatched) does the moving. Found along the way: the ring movers' y
  comparison borrows from the x comparison (no SEC between); the keep-range mover still tests for
  move 3, which never reaches it; the charge mover leaves X at enemy + 6 on a free step; the
  fixed-direction mover ends its last case with a BRA to the next instruction and skips the
  resume bookkeeping the other "done" paths do; `Battle_RunEnemyMover` compares before a store that
  would change nothing. `$9829` (move done) and `$98A7` (stepping) are named now.

- Bank $C1 matched on through the battlers' animation and status visuals ($C1:2F97–$C1:3713):
  the status animation picker and its 16-record status table, the round-robin pick for enemies,
  the status effect dispatch and its eight handlers (overlay sprites, colour cycle, palette
  tint), the enemy animation tick (enemies in three groups, selected by `$A4`), the start-frame
  draw, and the PC poses: battle start, service 8 (victory, inferred) and service 9 (defeat,
  inferred). Found along the way: the Unk2F path compares the applied effect with the animation
  id $12, so a PC's list restarts on every pick; status restarts write `$968C,X` for enemy slots
  past the 3-byte PC array (slots 8-10 hit the enemy timers); effect handler 1 skips the reset
  the others do; the enemy tick's DEC/INC of `$A4` cancel out and its lists are copied 8 long
  but wrap at 4; Battle_PoseStep tests `$5DDD` with both branches meeting; `$A4DC` (start
  frames) lies inside the overlay-frame array. The stub name `Battle_TickStatusEffectVisuals`
  ($C1:3714) looks wrong.

- Bank $C1 matched on from the targeting system: the frame wait `BattleSys_PumpFrames`
  ($C1:007E), battler boxes and their collision tests against the screen cell map and each other
  ($C1:283D–$C1:2985), the position queries of service 5 of the $C10045 API (nearest/farthest

  (an init sequence), per the independent review of MainInit.
- The two cross-bank message vectors `$CD002D` / `$CD0030` used by the enemy info panel.

## Decisions

- 2026-10-08: routine headers are checked mechanically. `HEADER`: an Entry and an Exit line
  (tables exempt; sub-entries point at their parent with `; header: see <Parent>`). `CALLERS`:
  every caller site `tools/xref.py` confirms is named in the header (address, containing
  routine, or a site count). Requested by the maintainer after review rounds kept rejecting
  routines for missing Entry/Exit lines and incomplete caller lists. The existing headers were
  brought up to it in a separate commit first; that edit changed 112 routines' source hashes, so
  their reviews no longer count until they are re-reviewed.

- 2026-10-08: jumps and calls (`JSR`, `JMP`, `JSL`, `JML`, branches) are exempt from the
  explicit-width rule; each has exactly one encoding, so its width can't drift with how a define
  is written. Approved by the maintainer after an independent review flagged that the exemption
  had been added inside a code revision. From now on, standard changes are their own commit
  (CONTRIBUTING.md, "Changing the standard").
