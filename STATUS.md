## Current target

The readability burn-down ([NEXT.md](NEXT.md)): every routine matched before the gate
(`tools/readability_baseline.txt`) is being brought up to [STYLE.md](STYLE.md), re-verified and
independently reviewed before anything new goes to `main`. Banks $00 and $FD are done; $C0 and
$C1 are in progress.

## Recent results

- The battle targeting system is matched end to end ($C1:1F79–$C1:283C): the 33-entry target-mode
  table and its 18 handlers, the area-query service, the candidate-list helpers, and the four
  area shapes (row band, party triangle, line, circle).
- `BattleMenu_EnqueueReadyBattler` (service 1 of the $C10045 API) puts a battler in the
  ATB-ready queue and plays the turn-ready cue.
- The readability gate: `tools/lint_readability.py` per routine, `tools/verify.py` proves every
  emitted byte against blank bases, `tools/progress.py` generates every number, reviews are
  recorded in `symbols/reviews.csv`.

## Known issues

- Hand-maintained totals in older notes overstated bank $C0 by 169 bytes; the generated numbers
  above are the correct ones.
- Two asar notes from earlier sessions were wrong: `.b`/`.w` alone reproduces the encodings
  that were hand-written as `db`, and `BRL` accepts a label.

  wrong; needs a trace.
- The two cross-bank message vectors `$CD002D` / `$CD0030` used by the enemy info panel.
